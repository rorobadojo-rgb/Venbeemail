## Which file do I use?

Pick the file that matches your frame or paper size.

- satwa-tropis-01_2x3_12x18in.jpg : 4x6, 8x12, 12x18 inches
- satwa-tropis-01_3x4_12x16in.jpg : 6x8, 9x12, 12x16 inches
- satwa-tropis-01_4x5_8x10in.jpg : 4x5, 8x10 inches
- satwa-tropis-01_11x14_11x14in.jpg : 11x14 inches
- satwa-tropis-01_iso_a3.jpg : A5, A4, A3 (international paper sizes)


Not sure? Measure the opening of your frame, then choose the file that lists that size.
If a file is inside a ZIP folder, double-click the ZIP to open it and you will find the image files inside.

## How to print

At home
- Use heavy matte paper or photo paper, and choose the highest quality setting on your printer.
- Select "actual size" or "fit to page" and check the preview before printing.

At a local print shop
- Bring the file on a USB drive or upload it on the shop's website.
- Ask for the size you need and a matte or lustre finish.

With an online print service
- Upload the file that matches your size, and check the crop preview before you order.

## About colors

Printed colors may vary slightly from what you see on screen, because every screen, printer, and paper is different.
We recommend a small test print before printing large.
